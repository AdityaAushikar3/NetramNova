1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & REFRAMED PITCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Problem in India:
  - 77 Million diabetic adults in India (2nd highest globally).
  - 18% prevalence of Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) (~14 million at risk of vision loss).
  - 1 Ophthalmologist per 100,000 rural population—mass manual screening is infeasible.

• The Real Operational Gaps in Existing Commercial Systems:
  Commercial AI systems (Medios AI by Remidio, 3nethra by Forus Health) are strong single-patient
  diagnostic tools, but districts face 3 operational challenges:
  1. Multi-Device Validation Gaps: Commercial AIs have demonstrated validation primarily on
     their own hardware ecosystems (Remidio Fundus-on-Phone and Forus 3nethra). PHCs using
     diverse third-party cameras or smartphone adapters lack open cross-camera validation.
  2. Binary Triage Constraints: Binary "Refer / No-Refer" systems treat high-confidence
     cases and borderline cases identically, missing an opportunity for uncertainty-guided triage.
  3. Lack of District Decision-Support Tools: Commercial products sell diagnostic software/devices,
     providing zero decision-support tools for District Health Officers (DHOs) to optimize camera
     allocation or doctor shift rosters across 50+ rural clinics.

• Our Solution (NetraOS):
  We do NOT claim to build a higher-accuracy classifier than established clinical products. We built
  NetraOS: the camera-agnostic triage and capacity-planning layer that sits on top of fundus
  imaging workflows, combining INSTANT EDGE-OFFLINE TRIAGE at the rural PHC with asynchronous
  district capacity planning.


2. THE 4 CORE PRODUCT PILLARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Pillar 1: Multi-Vendor Camera Generalization & Camera-Invariant AI Training
  - Supported Cameras: Forus 3nethra, Remidio Fundus-on-Phone, Topcon 3CCD, Zeiss Visucam, Smartphone Adapters (Volk).
  - Camera-Invariant Training: Pairs physical preprocessing (Foracchia + CLAHE) with multi-camera domain adaptation training
    (across APTOS, Messidor-2, EyePACS) so the AI model explicitly learns RETINAL DISEASE LESIONS rather than camera color styles.

• Pillar 2: 3-Tier Uncertainty-Calibrated Triage & Multi-Factor IQA Gate
  - Multi-Factor IQA Gate: Checks Focus (Blur), Illumination (Exposure), and FOV Coverage (Centering) before AI diagnosis.
    If defective, triggers immediate local recapture alert ("Photo too dark", "Retina cut off").
  - Tier 1: High Confidence (Conf > 90%) -> Auto-Cleared 100% OFFLINE ON-DEVICE AT PHC (<2.0s total end-to-end delay, 0 network usage).
  - Tier 2: Low Confidence / Borderline (70% <= Conf <= 90%) -> Asynchronously uploaded to PRIORITY Doctor Queue.
  - Tier 3: Out-of-Distribution / Ungradeable (Conf < 70%) -> Local Recapture Alert + Specialist Escalation.

• Pillar 3: Simulink District Resource & Capacity Optimizer
  SimEvents model calculating optimal camera allocation & doctor shift rosters across 50 PHCs
  for 100,000 annual screenings. (Cellular upload applies ONLY to the ~26% of cases needing doctor review).

• Pillar 4: 12-Month Longitudinal Progression Risk Engine
  Predicts 12-month disease progression risk to advise 3, 6, or 12 month follow-up recall intervals.

• Doctor Review Time (Tier-2 Priority): < 30 seconds per fast-track verification
• Derived Doctor Workload & Network Reduction: ~74% Doctor & Network Bandwidth Reduction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Total Screening Population = 100,000 Patients/Year
  ├── ~82% Level 0 (No DR) & Level 1 (Mild NPDR)
  │    └── ~90% High Confidence (Tier 1) ──► Instant Offline Auto-Clear (~73.8% of total, 0 network bandwidth)

        Workload & Network Bandwidth Reduction = 100% - 26.2% = ~73.8% ≈ 74% Total Reduction


6. PRECISION-ENGINEERED JURY Q&A DEFENSE SCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: "Medios AI and 3nethra already exist in India. How is your project different?"
Answer: "Medios AI and 3nethra have each only demonstrated their AI working reliably on their own
hardware ecosystem — neither has published cross-camera validation on a PHC's existing equipment.
We built NetraOS specifically to work across any fundus camera a district already owns, without asking
them to buy new hardware. Furthermore, we provide District Health Officers with Simulink capacity-planning
tools to manage doctor rosters across 50 clinics—something commercial device vendors do not offer."

Q2: "Why combine Foracchia et al. background normalization with camera-invariant AI training?"
Answer: "Foracchia et al. standardizes physical pixel lighting drift across camera sensors. We pair this with
camera-invariant domain adversarial AI training across multi-camera datasets (APTOS, Messidor-2, EyePACS),
ensuring the neural network explicitly learns RETINAL DISEASE LESIONS rather than camera-specific color styles."

Q3: "Doesn't network upload over 2G/3G make you slower in rural PHCs than Medios AI's offline model?"
Answer: "NetraOS runs AI inference 100% offline on-device at the rural PHC in under 2 seconds total end-to-end.
For ~74% of patients (Tier 1 High-Confidence Auto-Cleared), the patient receives their result instantly on-site
with zero network dependency. 2G/3G cellular upload applies ONLY to the ~26% of cases requiring human doctor
verification, reducing district network bandwidth demands by 74%."

Q4: "How did you derive the ~74% doctor workload and network reduction?"
Answer: "In general diabetic screening populations, roughly 82% of patients have Level 0 (No DR) or Level 1 (Mild DR).
Our Tier 1 high-confidence edge model auto-clears ~90% of these normal/mild cases offline (representing ~73.8% of total
screenings). Thus, only the remaining ~26.2% of cases (Tier 2 priority and Tier 3 OOD) require network upload and doctor
review, resulting in a ~74% reduction in doctor review volume and network bandwidth."
