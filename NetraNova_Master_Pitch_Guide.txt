1. THE PROBLEM (THE RURAL HEALTHCARE CRISIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Scale of the Crisis in India:
1. Preventable Blindness Scale: India has over 77 million diabetic patients. 
   Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) is the leading cause of preventable blindness in 
   working-age adults.
2. Rural Specialist Deficit: 85% of retina specialists reside in urban tier-1 
   hospitals, while 70% of diabetic patients live in rural villages.
3. Low-Quality Edge Capture: Rural Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANMs) using 
   handheld fundus cameras produce images with severe motion blur, flash glare, 
   and peripheral shadow falloff.

Why Existing AI Solutions Fail in Practice:
* Unexplainable Black-Box Heatmaps (Grad-CAM): Vague colored blobs that 
  retina specialists cannot clinically verify or trust.
* Peripheral False Positives: Generic contrast enhancement (CLAHE) blindly 
  boosts peripheral shadow falloff, turning dark edge noise into fake red 
  "speckles" and diagnosing fake microaneurysms.
* Disconnected Referral Pathways: Existing AI tools output a DR grade but 
  fail to link patients to actionable Anti-VEGF treatment pathways.
* Cloud Dependency: Heavy deep learning models require high-speed internet, 
  failing completely in rural PHCs with zero connectivity.


2. THE SOLUTION (NETRANOVA WORKSTATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NetraNova is an offline-first, camera-agnostic clinical decision-support 
workstation designed for rural Primary Health Centre (PHC) edge nodes.

* Target Users: Rural ANM / ASHA health workers & Remote Retina Specialists.
* Core Function: Empowers ANMs to perform 1.34-second specialist-grade 
  triage using existing low-cost fundus cameras with Rs. 0 hardware overhead.
* Key Innovation: Combines Foracchia illumination normalization, an automated 
  Edge Quality Gate, dual spectral viewports, and the ETDRS 4-2-1 spatial audit 
  rule with automated Anti-VEGF referral triage.
