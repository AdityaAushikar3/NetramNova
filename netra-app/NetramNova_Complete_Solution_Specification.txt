1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
India is the diabetes capital of the world with over 77 million diabetic adults.
Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) affects approximately 18% of this population and is a
leading cause of preventable adult blindness. Early screening can prevent 90% of
severe vision loss, yet India has only ~1 ophthalmologist per 100,000 rural citizens.
Mass manual fundus screening is clinically impossible.

Existing commercial AI systems operate as black boxes, lack clinical explainability,
and fail drastically when confronted with blurry, non-mydriatic images taken by
low-cost portable fundus cameras in rural Primary Healthcare Centres (PHCs).

NetramNova is an edge-native, clinical-grade retinal screening workstation that
solves this through a 6-stage engineering pipeline:
  • Sub-300ms on-device quality triage (rejecting inadequate scans immediately)
  • Physics-based photometric and color space normalization (Foracchia + L*a*b*)
  • Parallel multi-task deep learning (EfficientNet-B2 grading + U-Net lesion masks)
  • Dual-layer explainability (Grad-CAM heatmaps + ETDRS 4-2-1 clinical rules)
  • Automated CSME foveal threat proximity triage
  • Offline edge deployment via INT8-quantized ONNX Runtime with store-and-forward sync


================================================================================
2. DETAILED 6-STAGE TECHNICAL PIPELINE
================================================================================

STAGE 1: EDGE CAPTURE & QUALITY GATE (PRE-INFERENCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Hardware Input: Non-mydriatic, portable fundus cameras (Forus 3nethra, Remidio).
• Quality Assessment: OpenCV Wasm module executing locally in < 300 ms.
• Evaluated Metrics:
    - Focus Sharpness: Laplacian variance score (Threshold >= 60.0). Rejects defocus
      and patient motion saccades.
    - Glare Saturation: Overexposed pixel percentage with intensity > 250
      (Threshold <= 8.0%). Detects corneal reflection artifacts.
    - Field of View (FOV): Retinal circle area coverage vs bounding box (>= 35.0%).
• Decision Logic:
    - FAIL -> Halts inference immediately; triggers visual recapture prompt.
    - PASS -> Routes forward to Stage 2.

STAGE 2: PHOTOMETRIC STANDARDIZATION & COLOR SCIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Foracchia 2D Illumination Normalization:
    - Extracts green spectral channel (540-570 nm), where hemoglobin has peak optical
      absorption and vascular contrast is maximal.
    - Fits a macroscopic Gaussian-smoothed illumination surface S(x, y).
    - Normalizes intensity: I_norm(x, y) = [I(x, y) / (S(x, y) + eps)] * mean(S).
    - Flattens uneven flash gradients and camera lens vignetting.
• CIE L*a*b* Luminance-Decoupled Contrast Enhancement:
    - Converts standardized image from BGR to CIE L*a*b* space.
    - Applies mild CLAHE (ClipLimit = 0.01, TileGrid = 8x8) strictly to L* (Luminance).
    - Preserves a* (red/green) and b* (blue/yellow) chrominance untouched to eliminate
      color distortion of true lesions.
    - Neutralizes dark choroidal background variations common in Indian retinas.
    - Reconstructs clean RGB image for neural network transfer learning.

STAGE 3: PARALLEL EDGE AI INFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Engine A: DR Severity Classifier (EfficientNet-B2)
    - Architecture: Pretrained EfficientNet-B2 with custom classification head.
    - Output: Multi-class probabilities for all 5 international ICDR grades:
        * Grade 0: No DR (normal retina)
        * Grade 1: Mild NPDR (microaneurysms only)
        * Grade 2: Moderate NPDR (exudates, hemorrhages)
        * Grade 3: Severe NPDR (4-2-1 rule vascular abnormalities)
        * Grade 4: Proliferative DR (neovascularization, vitreous hemorrhage)
    - Optimization: Inverse-frequency class weighting to penalize false negatives
      on rare severe grades; Cosine Annealing with Warmup scheduling.
• Engine B: Lesion Segmentation & Landmark Localization
    - Multi-Class Segmentation: U-Net with patch-based overlapping sliding window
      (256x256 tiles with Gaussian blending) to resolve sub-pixel microaneurysms.
      Interchangeable YOLOv8-Seg wrapper for larger hemorrhages and exudates.
    - Anatomical Landmark Head: EfficientNet-B0 regression predicting normalized
      (x, y) coordinates for Optic Disc center and Foveal center.

STAGE 4: DUAL-LAYER EXPLAINABILITY FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Visual Layer (Grad-CAM):
    - Generates gradient-weighted class activation heatmaps from the final
      convolutional layers of EfficientNet-B2.
    - Overlays calibrated thermal maps highlighting exact clusters that triggered
      the grade prediction.
• Algorithmic Clinical Layer (ETDRS 4-2-1 Rule Engine):
    - Divides fundus into 4 anatomical quadrants centered on the fovea:
      Superior, Inferior, Nasal, Temporal.
    - Clinically validates the gold-standard 4-2-1 rule:
        * >= 20 intraretinal hemorrhages in all 4 quadrants
        * Definite venous beading in >= 2 quadrants
        * Prominent Intraretinal Microvascular Abnormalities (IRMA) in >= 1 quadrant
    - Directly bridges deep learning outputs with ophthalmological diagnostic criteria.

STAGE 5: CSME FOVEAL PROXIMITY & REFERRAL TRIAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Clinically Significant Macular Edema (CSME) Risk Engine:
    - Computes Euclidean pixel distance between detected hard exudate clusters
      and the foveal coordinate.
    - CSME Threshold: Exudates within < 1.0 Disc Diameter (~1500 microns) of the fovea.
• Referral Triage Classification:
    - URGENT REFERRAL TICKET:
        * Any Grade 3 (Severe NPDR) or Grade 4 (PDR).
        * Any patient with Foveal-Involved CSME risk.
        * Flags patient for priority Anti-VEGF injection (Aflibercept/Ranibizumab)
          or Pan-Retinal Photocoagulation (PRP) laser therapy.
    - ROUTINE SCREENING REPORT:
        * Grade 0, Grade 1, or Grade 2 without macular threat.
        * Prescribes lifestyle counseling and 6 to 12 month follow-up screening.

STAGE 6: OFFLINE EDGE DEPLOYMENT & EDGE SYNC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Universal CPU Deployment:
    - Models exported to ONNX format with INT8 dynamic post-training quantization.
    - Runs inference in < 1.5 seconds per image on standard dual-core PHC laptops.
    - Zero cloud or internet dependency during the screening session.
• Store-and-Forward Edge Synchronization:
    - AES-256 encrypted SQLite database stores patient demographics and tickets locally.
    - Background sync daemon automatically pushes encrypted packages to district
      tele-ophthalmology servers as soon as intermittent 3G/4G connectivity is detected.
